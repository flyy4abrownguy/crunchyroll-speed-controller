Control video playback speed on Crunchyroll and HIDIVE — from 0.25x to 4.0x.

FEATURES:
• Slider + quick presets (0.5x, 0.75x, 1.0x, 1.25x, 1.5x, 2.0x)
• Auto-skip intros, outros, recaps and credits — switch it off any time
• Keyboard shortcuts — control speed without leaving fullscreen
• On-screen indicator with auto-hide
• Customizable speed step — choose 0.05x, 0.10x, or 0.25x increments
• Per-series speed — remembers a different speed for each show
• Remembers your preferred speed across sessions
• Send Feedback — built-in form to report bugs or request features
• Dark theme matching Crunchyroll's aesthetic
• Privacy-first: no tracking, no analytics, no account required

YOUR WATCH STATS:
See exactly how much time you've reclaimed — a 30-day activity chart, your current
and longest watch streak, which series have eaten the most hours, and badges that
unlock as your saved time adds up. All of it stays in local storage on your own
machine and never leaves your browser.

KEYBOARD SHORTCUTS (all rebindable):
Shift + > / < — Increase / Decrease speed
Shift + ? — Reset to 1.0x
Shift + V — Toggle indicator
Alt + . / , — Global increase / decrease
Alt + 0 — Global reset
Alt + V — Global toggle indicator

Global shortcuts work even when the page isn't focused, and can be changed at
chrome://extensions/shortcuts.

Lightweight, open source, and built for anime fans. Free, with an optional tip jar
